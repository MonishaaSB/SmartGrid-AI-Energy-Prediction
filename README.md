# SmartGrid AI – Predictive Energy Intelligence System

## Project Overview
SmartGrid AI is a Machine Learning-based energy monitoring project that analyses historical electricity data, predicts future energy demand, and detects abnormal energy consumption patterns.
The project aims to support smarter energy planning and help identify unusual electricity usage.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Python** – Core programming language
- **Pandas** – Data cleaning and manipulation
- **NumPy** – Numerical operations
- **Matplotlib** – Data visualization
- **Scikit-learn** – Machine Learning models
- **Google Colab** – Development environment

## Problems Identified
- Traditional energy monitoring mainly focuses on current and historical usage.
- Unexpected changes in electricity consumption may go unnoticed.
- Future energy demand is difficult to estimate accurately without predictive analysis.
- Unusual energy consumption patterns can affect energy planning.

## Proposed Solutions
- **Demand Forecasting:** Uses Random Forest Regression to predict upcoming electricity demand.
- **Anomaly Detection:** Uses Isolation Forest to identify unusual energy consumption patterns.
- **Data Analysis:** Examines historical electricity data to understand usage trends.
- **Performance Evaluation:** Uses MAE, RMSE and R² to evaluate prediction performance.

## Traditional Monitoring vs SmartGrid AI

### Traditional Energy Monitoring:

-Focuses mainly on past and current electricity usage.
-Relies on manual identification of unusual readings.
-Provides limited predictive insights.
-Often follows a reactive monitoring approach.

### SmartGrid AI:

-Predicts future electricity demand using Machine Learning.
-Automatically identifies abnormal consumption patterns.
-Provides data-driven predictive insights.
-Supports proactive energy planning and monitoring.
